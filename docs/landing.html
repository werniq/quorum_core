Quorum

Define what each critical workflow should do. Quorum checks whether it ran, whether its reported volume stayed within the expected range, and how strong the evidence actually is.

Open source, zero telemetry, and designed so your workflow data can stay in your infrastructure.

Reliability evidence that protects client retainers and supports proactive maintenance reporting.

Feature availability

Capability Status
Contract Catalog + n8n heartbeat contracts Available
HubSpot webinar registrations → Zoom webinar registrants Preview
Zapier / Make / verification for all workflows Planned

Required limitation: Heartbeat and volume-band evidence can be self-reported. They do not independently prove destination delivery.

Outcome verification is Preview and limited to HubSpot webinar registrations → Zoom webinar registrants — not every n8n workflow. Quorum does not claim to stop incidents from occurring.

Allowed claim (heartbeat): Quorum shows what your n8n workflows are expected to do and alerts you when they fail, stop reporting, or produce an unacceptable result.
